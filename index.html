Frequently asked questions

How can I order from ARLA?

Tap “Ready to Order?” to browse the full menu and build your cart. Online checkout is not active yet, so please call ARLA or message us to place an order.

Where is pickup available?

Pickup is in Gaithersburg, Maryland. Contact ARLA directly for pickup details.

Can ARLA prepare food or a buffet for my event?

The buffet is $15.99, and children under 5 eat free with a paying adult. Contact ARLA to discuss event food and confirm details.

Can I book ARLA for a gathering?

Get in touch with ARLA about your gathering and ask about the space and availability.

Is delivery available?

Online delivery is not currently configured on this website. Please contact ARLA to ask about delivery before placing an order.
